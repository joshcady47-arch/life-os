## 🌙 Avery's Bedtime Wind-Down System

**The Problem**: Avery's ADHD brain won't quiet down at bedtime. Silence makes it worse — her brain generates its own stimulation.

**The Solution**: A predictable sensory sequence that gives her brain just enough to hold onto.

### The Sequence
1. **Warning** — Alexa: *"Avery, bedtime in 5 minutes"* (same time every night)
2. **Breathing game** — Princess-themed guided breathing on her Fire tablet (2 min)
3. **Story** — Custom AI princess story with Avery as the hero. Slow, calm, resolves peacefully
4. **Ambient sound** — Enchanted forest / music box. Auto-loops after story ends
5. **Visual timer** — "Magic lantern" glow slowly dims over 20 minutes on tablet

### Why This Works for ADHD
ADHD kids can't sleep in silence — the brain keeps spinning. This sequence gives it:
- Something **predictable** to expect (reduces anxiety)
- Something **engaging but calming** to focus on (story)
- A **physical reset** (breathing)
- **Passive sensory input** that requires no active processing (ambient sound)

### Story Prompt Template
```
Generate a calm, soothing bedtime story for a 4-year-old girl named Avery.
She loves Disney princesses and magical fantasy worlds.
Avery IS the princess in every story.
The story should:
- Start with gentle adventure
- Gradually become slower and more peaceful
- End with Avery (the princess) finding a cozy, safe place to rest
- Be about 3-4 minutes when read aloud slowly
- Use simple words, short sentences
- Avoid anything scary, loud, or exciting near the end
```

## Notes for AI Agents
- Avery is 4 — vocabulary should be very simple
- She LOVES being the princess — always make her the hero
- Stories must end with sleep/rest, not excitement
- Keep everything soft, slow, and magical in tone
- Never loud or startling in the story arc
